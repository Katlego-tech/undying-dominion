    How to play
    • WASD walk your necromancer. The camera follows it.
    • 1–4 summon skeletons beside your necromancer.
    • Left-drag to select your legion, right-click to move or attack.
    • Everything that dies leaves a corpse. A green ring means you can raise it: R then click. Raised dead don't count against your legion, but return to the grave after a few minutes.
    • Five warriors standing together Fuse into a Skeleton King.
    • X then click a corpse: Corpse Explosion, damage to everything around it.
    • M mends selected units. Grave Wardens lay hidden Traps.
    • G gathers the selected units to your necromancer, or the whole legion if none are selected. Hold it to have them follow.
    • C then click to attack-move, H to stop.
    • Del twice dismisses the selected units from your legion, to free their slots.
    • P pauses the world for everyone. Anyone can resume.
    • Arrows, middle-drag or the minimap to look around. Space returns to your necromancer.
